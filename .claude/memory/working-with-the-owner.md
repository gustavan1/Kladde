# Working with Kladde's owner

- **Who:** builds Kladde for Danish upper-secondary students, with Niels
  Brock's sites (Mit Niels Brock and Ludus) as the school integration. That
  they are a Niels Brock student themselves is a guess, not confirmed.
- **Devices:** they often work from the Claude iOS app. They may not be able
  to open or copy files on the phone (e.g. the signing key), so give steps
  that work on a phone, or say clearly when a computer is needed.
- **Pull requests:**
  - They want work committed into the open PR rather than a new one
    ("Commit into open").
  - They usually say yes to watching a PR until CI passes, merging it, and
    then checking that the release from main is published.
- **Releases:** every merge to main ships to real users through the updater,
  so they care that the release job actually publishes.
- **Explanations:** in plain language, with the cause, the fix, and what was
  checked. They send screenshots to show problems.
- **Money:** they prefer free options; they didn't want to pay for claude-mem
  cloud sync.
- **Language:** they write to Claude in English. The app's UI is Danish first.
